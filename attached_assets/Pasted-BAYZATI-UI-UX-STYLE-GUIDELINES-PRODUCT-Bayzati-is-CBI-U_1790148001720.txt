BAYZATI — UI/UX STYLE GUIDELINES

PRODUCT
Bayzati is CBI UAE's modern financial planning and financial literacy application.

The product helps users understand their money, plan spending, build savings goals, improve financial habits, and learn financial concepts through simple personalized guidance.

The experience should feel like:
MODERN FINTECH + TRUSTED BANK + PERSONAL FINANCIAL COACH

It should NOT look like a traditional banking portal, accounting system, corporate dashboard, or children's educational app.


────────────────────────────────────
1. BRAND PERSONALITY
────────────────────────────────────

Bayzati should feel:

• Modern
• Intelligent
• Trustworthy
• Calm
• Friendly
• Premium
• Simple
• Encouraging
• Financially empowering
• UAE-focused
• Young, but not childish

The user should feel that financial planning has become easier and less intimidating.

Avoid:
• Corporate banking clutter
• Excessive financial jargon
• Overly playful/gamified visuals
• Neon fintech aesthetics
• Crypto-style interfaces
• Excessive gradients
• Excessive shadows
• Glassmorphism
• Dense dashboards

────────────────────────────────────
7. SPACING SYSTEM
────────────────────────────────────

Use an 8px-based spacing system.

4px  = micro
8px  = small
12px = compact
16px = standard
24px = section
32px = large
48px = major
64px = page separation

Favor whitespace.

Do not try to fill every empty area.

The interface should breathe.


────────────────────────────────────
8. BORDER RADIUS
────────────────────────────────────

Small:
8px

Buttons / Inputs:
12px

Standard cards:
16–18px

Feature cards:
20–24px

Pills:
999px

Do not make every component extremely rounded.

────────────────────────────────────
14. HOME DASHBOARD
────────────────────────────────────

The dashboard should answer these questions in order:

1. HOW AM I DOING?
2. WHERE IS MY MONEY GOING?
3. AM I ON TRACK?
4. WHAT SHOULD I DO NEXT?
5. WHAT CAN I LEARN?

Recommended hierarchy:

Greeting

Financial Health

Income / Spending / Available Money

Monthly Budget

Savings Goals

Personalized Insight

AI Assistant

Learning Recommendation


Do not create a traditional banking dashboard filled with balances, tables and charts.


────────────────────────────────────
15. FINANCIAL HEALTH
────────────────────────────────────

Financial Health should be one of the strongest visual elements.

Example:

Financial Health

78
GOOD

↑ 6 points this month

Use a clean circular indicator, progress ring, or simple gauge.

Avoid complicated speedometer-style charts.

Explain WHY the score changed.


────────────────────────────────────
16. BUDGET DESIGN
────────────────────────────────────

Budgets should be visual and easy to understand.

Example:

Monthly Budget

AED 3,240 remaining

████████████░░░░

68% used

Never force the user to calculate what numbers mean.

Always provide interpretation.


────────────────────────────────────
17. GOALS
────────────────────────────────────

Goals should feel motivating but mature.

Example:

Travel Fund

AED 6,800
of AED 10,000

68%

█████████████░░░

AED 3,200 to go


Show:
• Goal name
• Current amount
• Target
• Progress
• Remaining amount
• Optional target date


────────────────────────────────────
18. CHARTS
────────────────────────────────────

Keep charts SIMPLE.

Users should understand the chart within approximately 3 seconds.

Color hierarchy:

Primary financial data:
#003B73

Secondary:
#139BE8

Highlighted:
#D20A58

Positive:
#12A66A

Warning:
#F5A623

Critical:
#D92D20


Prefer:
• Donut charts
• Simple bar charts
• Progress bars
• Line charts
• Trend indicators

Avoid:
• 3D charts
• Complex legends
• Too many categories
• Rainbow charts
• Unnecessary gridlines


────────────────────────────────────
19. AI ASSISTANT
────────────────────────────────────

The Bayzati AI assistant should feel like:

A knowledgeable financial guide,
NOT a generic chatbot.

Its UI should use:

Bayzati Navy
Bayzati Blue
Small Magenta accents

Give the AI a subtle visual identity distinct from ordinary app screens.

Suggested AI button:

✦ Ask [AI NAME]

Example questions:

"Can I afford this?"

"Where did I spend the most this month?"

"Help me save AED 10,000."

"Explain credit cards to me."

"How much should my emergency fund be?"


AI answers should:

• Use simple language
• Explain reasoning
• Highlight important numbers
• Suggest clear next steps
• Avoid unnecessarily technical financial terminology


────────────────────────────────────
20. FINANCIAL LITERACY
────────────────────────────────────

The Learn section should NOT look like Moodle, Blackboard, or an LMS.

Avoid:

Chapter 1
Chapter 2
Chapter 3

Instead organize learning around real financial situations.

Examples:

STARTING OUT

Understanding Your Salary

BUILDING GOOD HABITS

Creating Your First Budget

USING CREDIT

How Credit Cards Actually Work

PLANNING AHEAD

Building an Emergency Fund


Whenever possible, connect learning to user data.

BAD:

"Learn About Budgeting"


BETTER:

"You've used 68% of your monthly budget."

"Learn how the 50/30/20 method could help you plan the remaining AED 3,240."


────────────────────────────────────
21. CONTENT STYLE
────────────────────────────────────

Write like a helpful financial coach.

NOT like a bank policy document.

BAD:

Your expenditure allocation has exceeded the recommended threshold.


GOOD:

You've spent more than planned on dining this month.


BAD:

Optimize your savings allocation.


GOOD:

Putting aside AED 500 more this month would keep your goal on track.


Use:
• Short sentences
• Plain English
• Clear numbers
• Direct recommendations
• Helpful explanations


────────────────────────────────────
22. UAE LOCALIZATION
────────────────────────────────────

Default currency:

AED

Format consistently:

AED 2,500

AED 12,450.75

Support both:
English
Arabic

RTL layouts must be properly mirrored.

Do not simply right-align English UI components.


────────────────────────────────────
23. MOBILE DESIGN
────────────────────────────────────

Design MOBILE FIRST.

Primary mobile navigation:

Home
Plan
Goals
Learn
Profile

Minimum touch target:
44px

Keep important financial information visible without excessive scrolling.

Primary actions should be reachable with one hand where practical.


────────────────────────────────────
24. RESPONSIVENESS
────────────────────────────────────

Mobile:
Single-column cards

Tablet:
1–2 columns

Desktop:
Use a restrained grid.

Recommended desktop content max width:
1200–1280px

Do not stretch financial cards across huge monitors.


────────────────────────────────────
25. ACCESSIBILITY
────────────────────────────────────

Maintain WCAG-friendly contrast.

Never communicate meaning using color alone.

Example:

Do NOT show only:
Red bar

Instead show:

Budget exceeded
AED 340 over budget

+ visual warning indicator


All interactive elements require visible focus states.


────────────────────────────────────
26. MOTION
────────────────────────────────────

Animations should feel subtle and premium.

Use approximately:
150–300ms transitions

Suitable animations:

• Progress bars filling
• Financial health score changing
• Goal completion
• Card appearance
• AI typing/loading
• Navigation transitions

Avoid:
• Bouncing components
• Constant animations
• Excessive confetti
• Game-like effects

────────────────────────────────────
29. CORE DESIGN RULE
────────────────────────────────────

Whenever choosing between:

MORE FINANCIAL INFORMATION

and

EASIER FINANCIAL UNDERSTANDING

always choose:

EASIER FINANCIAL UNDERSTANDING.


Bayzati should make someone who is NOT financially knowledgeable feel confident using the application.


FINAL VISUAL TARGET

The finished product should look like a polished consumer fintech product created specifically for CBI UAE.

It should combine:

THE TRUST OF A BANK
+
THE SIMPLICITY OF A MODERN FINTECH APP
+
THE GUIDANCE OF A PERSONAL FINANCIAL COACH

Every new screen must follow this design system.
Do not introduce new colors, typography, icon styles, card styles, or UI patterns without a functional reason.
